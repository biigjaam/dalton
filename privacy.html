DALTON privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

DALTON is a personal assistant app used only by its developer, Jamie Hill. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, DALTON reads, sends and organises email in Gmail and reads and changes events in Google Calendar, for that account only.

Where data goes

Data from Google is processed on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never shared. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not transferred to anyone else, except that the owner's own requests may be processed by the AI service he uses to run the assistant.

DALTON's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
